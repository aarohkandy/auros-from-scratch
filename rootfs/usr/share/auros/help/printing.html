Printing

Most printers made in the last ten years work by themselves: one on your wifi, or one plugged in with its cable.

Print something

  1. Switch the printer on, and check it has paper.
  2. In the program you’re using, press Print. It is often in a menu, or a picture of a printer. Or hold down Ctrl and press P.
  3. A window shows your printers. Press yours. (“Print to File” isn’t a printer: it makes a copy on the computer.)
  4. Press Print.

If your printer isn’t in the list

  • A wifi printer must be on the same wifi as this computer. Its own screen or its instructions say how to join it to your wifi. Then wait a minute and try again.
  • A printer with a cable: plug its cable into this computer, wait a minute, and try again.
  • In your programs, Printers shows every printer this computer knows about, and can look for one.
  • Still not there? Some older printers need extra help. Ask someone who knows computers, or see Getting help below.

Getting help

AurOS has no phone line. The people who make AurOS answer questions on the internet: someone you trust can ask for you on the AurOS project page on GitHub, under “Issues”. Never post passwords or private details there: everyone can read it.

When you have finished reading, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
