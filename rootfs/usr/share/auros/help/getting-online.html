Getting online

Your computer gets online through your wifi, or through a cable from your internet box.

Join your wifi

  1. At the bottom of the screen, press Internet.
  2. Wait a moment while it says “Looking for wifi near you”. A list of names appears.
  3. Press the name of your wifi. It is usually printed on a sticker on the bottom or the back of your internet box, often beside the words “Network” or “Wi-Fi name”.
  4. Type the password. It is usually on the same sticker, often called “Key” or “Password”. Capital letters matter, so copy it exactly. Then press Connect.
  5. It says “You are connected”. That’s it: your computer remembers, and joins by itself next time.

If it doesn’t work

  • “That password did not work”: type it again, slowly. A zero and the letter O look alike; so do the number 1, a small L and a capital I.
  • Your wifi isn’t in the list: move closer to the internet box, check its lights are on, and press Internet again.
  • “This computer has no wifi”: use a cable instead (below).

Use a cable

Plug a network cable from your internet box into the matching socket on this computer. Your computer gets online by itself within a minute. Nothing to type.

Is it working?

Open Firefox, the program for websites, from your home screen. If a page appears, you’re online.

When you have finished reading, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
