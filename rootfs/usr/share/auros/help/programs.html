Getting a new program

New programs come from Get more programs. They are free, and you don’t need a password.

Find Get more programs

Its picture is a shopping bag. Where it is depends on the look you chose:

  • Like Windows: press All Programs on the bar; it is the last one in the list.
  • Like a Mac: it is the button next to Find.
  • Big and simple: it is on your home page.
  • One thing at a time: it is the last big button on the first page.

Get a program

  1. Press Get more programs. The first time, it can take a minute or two to fill up.
  2. Press the magnifying glass at the top, and type the name of the program you want, for example VLC for playing videos.
  3. Press the program in the list.
  4. Press Install. Wait while it is fetched; a big program takes a few minutes.
  5. A message says it is ready. Press the message, or press Open, to start it. From now on it is with your other programs too.

You need to be online to get a program. Getting online shows how.

Remove a program

In Get more programs, press Installed at the top, then Uninstall next to the program.

Programs from websites

Programs made for Windows don’t work here, and AurOS doesn’t put programs on this computer from files you download. If a website offers you a file to install, look for the same program in Get more programs instead. That is safer, and it keeps itself up to date.

If anyone asks you to get a program so they can “see your screen” or “fix your computer”, don’t. Unless you called them yourself and you know them, it is a trick. See AurOS will never call you.
