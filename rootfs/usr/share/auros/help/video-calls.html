Video calls

Most video calls work in Firefox, the program for websites. Someone sends you a link, you press it, and you’re in.

Join a call from a link

  1. Open the email or message with the link in it, and press the link. Firefox opens the call.
  2. If the page offers to download a program, look for the words “Join from your browser” and press those instead.
  3. Firefox asks whether the website may use your camera and microphone. If you were expecting the call, press Allow.
  4. Type your name if it asks, then press the button to join.

This works for Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Jitsi and many others. Some, like Zoom, also have their own program in Get more programs.

If they can’t see or hear you

  • Look on the call page for a crossed-out microphone or camera, and press it to switch it back on.
  • If you pressed Block by mistake: press the small camera picture at the left end of the address bar at the top of Firefox, remove the block, then press the round arrow to load the page again.
  • Check the camera isn’t covered. Some laptops have a little slider over it.

If you can’t hear them

  • Press Settings at the bottom of the screen, and make How loud the sound is louder.
  • Wireless headphones: in Settings, press Headphones and mice to connect them.

Only allow the camera and microphone for a call you were expecting. A website that suddenly asks, when you weren’t making a call, doesn’t need them: press Block.

When you have finished reading, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
